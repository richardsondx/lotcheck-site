Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

What we collect

  • On your device: your onboarding role, free-check balance, saved lots, referral code, and (optionally) your email address. This stays on your iPhone.
  • When you check a lot: the address you search. Used to compute the verdict and improve address matching. Not sold.
  • When you subscribe to Toronto lot alerts: your email address, stored by our newsletter provider (Dispatch) to send the alerts you asked for.
  • Purchases: handled entirely by Apple. We see subscription status (active/cancelled) via Superwall/StoreKit, never your card details.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't track you across other apps or websites. We don't use advertising SDKs.

Toronto lot alerts

If you enter your email for alerts, we use it only for those emails. Every email has an unsubscribe link; unsubscribing deletes you from the list. Double opt-in: you confirm via email before receiving anything.

Data retention

Device data is deleted when you delete the app. Alert-list emails are kept until you unsubscribe. Aggregated, non-identifying usage counts may be kept for analytics.

Your rights

To access or delete data we hold about you, email hello@getlotcheck.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

We'll post updates here and note the new date above.
