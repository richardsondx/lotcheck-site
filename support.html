Which cities does LotCheck cover?

Toronto only, under Zoning By-law 569-2013. Addresses outside Toronto are rejected before a check is counted, so you never waste a free check.

How do the approval odds work?

We analyzed 33,992 Committee of Adjustment decisions and compute the approval rate for your proposal type in your area. It is a statistical screening signal, not a guarantee and not legal advice.

How do the 3 free checks work?

Every install gets 3 full lot checks, no credit card required. After that, LotCheck Pro ($41.99/month or $279.99/year with a 7-day trial on annual) unlocks unlimited checks.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → LotCheck → Cancel. Access continues until the end of the billing period.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To leave the Toronto lot alerts email list, use the unsubscribe link in any email. For anything else, email us below.
