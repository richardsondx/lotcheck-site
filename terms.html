What LotCheck is

LotCheck is an iPhone app that summarizes publicly available City of Toronto zoning information and Committee of Adjustment decisions for Toronto lots. It is a screening tool, not professional advice.

Not a substitute for professionals

Verdicts are computed from public records and statistical patterns. They do not replace a licensed planner, architect, engineer, or lawyer. Confirm anything you act on with a qualified professional before making an offer, filing an application, or starting construction.

Coverage

LotCheck covers the City of Toronto only. Addresses outside Toronto will be rejected before a check is counted.

Free checks

Each device gets 3 free full lot checks, no credit card required. Unused checks do not roll over or transfer.
