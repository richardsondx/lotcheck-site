FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which areas does LotCheck cover?

Toronto only, for now. Every address is verified against the city's data before a check runs, so you'll know immediately if a lot is outside coverage. That check is free.

Where does the data come from?

Toronto's zoning bylaws for the envelope, and 33,992 decided Committee of Adjustment variances for the approval odds and timelines. It's the same public record a planner would pull, computed in seconds instead of days.

What do I get for free?

Three complete lot checks, including the zoning envelope, approval odds, laneway check, and development-charge estimate, plus shareable report links. No credit card required.

Is this a substitute for a planner or lawyer?

No. LotCheck tells you what's likely before you spend money on professionals. Confirm anything you act on with a licensed planner.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, anytime, from your iPhone settings. The annual plan is billed upfront and is non-refundable; cancelling stops the next renewal.
